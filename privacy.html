Privacy Policy — VIP Table Booking Pilot

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the VIP Table Booking Pilot, operated by Diton Pashaj as a sole proprietor ("we," "us"), collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our SMS ("text message") table booking and concierge service.

Information We Collect

When you text our phone number, we collect your mobile phone number, the content of your messages, and any booking details you provide (such as name, date, party size, and table preference) in order to respond to your inquiries and process reservations.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information solely to respond to your questions, process table bookings, generate payment links, and send you confirmations related to your reservation. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing purposes without your separate consent.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your conversation with us — typically a handful of messages per booking interaction. We do not send recurring marketing messages unless you separately opt in.

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your information is used only to operate this texting service, including through service providers (such as our messaging platform and payment processor) strictly to deliver the service you requested.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Consent Is Not a Condition of Purchase

You are never required to opt in to text messages in order to book a table or complete a payment with us. If you'd rather not receive text messages, you can reach us by email at ditonp@gmail.com to ask about availability and arrange a reservation instead. Opting out of future messages by replying STOP does not cancel or affect any reservation you have already confirmed and paid for.

Opting Out

You can opt out of receiving messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

We retain conversation and booking records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and comply with legal or business recordkeeping obligations.

Contact Us

This Program is operated by Diton Pashaj (sole proprietor). If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please text our business number directly, or email ditonp@gmail.com.
