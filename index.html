This is a pilot SMS-based concierge service for nightclub VIP table bookings, operated by Diton Pashaj as a sole proprietor. It's being tested on a limited basis to validate the concept ahead of a broader platform for nightclubs to offer this service to their own guests.

+1 (202) 875-8563

Text this number to ask about VIP table availability and pricing, or to book and pay for a table. An AI-assisted concierge will respond, answer your questions, and — if you're ready to book — send you a secure payment link to reserve your table.

How opting in works

By texting this number, you agree to receive SMS messages related to your inquiry, including responses to your questions and, if you book, confirmation and payment messages. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your conversation. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, or HELP for help.

Consent is not required to book

You are never required to opt in to text messages in order to book a table or complete a payment. If you'd rather not text, you can email ditonp@gmail.com to ask about availability and arrange a reservation instead. Opting out of future messages by replying STOP does not cancel or affect any reservation you've already confirmed and paid for.
